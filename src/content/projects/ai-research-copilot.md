AI Research CoPilot is a voice-first assistant that pairs Pipecat's real-time audio pipeline with Gemini 2.5 to answer research questions conversationally rather than through a text chat interface.

## Highlights

- Built for and demoed at the AI Tinkerers Gemini x Pipecat virtual hackathon (2025).
- Uses Next.js for the app shell and Pipecat for the low-latency voice pipeline.
- Last active in late 2025 — an earlier project relative to the rest of this catalog, but still one of the two projects the GitHub profile calls out under "Building Voice-First AI Systems."
